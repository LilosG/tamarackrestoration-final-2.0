## Seasonal Preparedness: Avoiding Sewage Emergencies

While emergency response is our expertise, prevention saves money and stress. Before the winter rainy season (October–March), consider:

- **Lateral inspection**: A $300–500 camera inspection identifies root intrusion or cracks before they cause a backup.
- **Grease disposal**: Never pour cooking grease down drains. It solidifies in pipes and traps debris.
- **Tree root management**: If you have large trees within 10 feet of your sewer lateral, consult an arborist about root barriers or strategic removal.
- **Septic pumping**: If you're on septic, pump every 3–5 years. A $400 pump prevents a $15,000+ emergency.
