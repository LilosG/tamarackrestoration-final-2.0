## Understanding Water Damage Categories

Not all water is the same. Restoration protocols depend on contamination level:

**Category 1 (Clean Water):** Burst pipes, overflowing bathtubs, or rain leaks. Low health risk but still requires fast drying to prevent mold.

**Category 2 (Gray Water):** Washing machine overflows, dishwasher leaks, or toilet overflow (with urine only). Contains microorganisms; requires antimicrobial treatment and careful handling.

**Category 3 (Black Water):** Sewage backups, toilet overflow with feces, or floodwater. Highly contaminated; requires professional sewage cleanup, full PPE, and strict containment protocols.

San Marcos homeowners dealing with Category 2 or 3 contamination should never attempt DIY cleanup. [Sewage cleanup](/services/sewage-cleanup/) requires licensed professionals with hazmat training and specialized equipment.

Understanding [emergency water damage restoration carlsbad first 60 minutes](/blog/emergency-water-damage-restoration-carlsbad-first-60-minutes/) principles applies directly to San Marcos. The critical early actions are:

1. **Stop the water source** (if safe and identifiable)
2. **Extract standing water** to prevent saturation of subfloors and framing
3. **Assess contamination** to determine appropriate PPE and cleanup protocols
4. **Document damage** with photos and video for insurance
5. **Begin air circulation** to prevent stagnant moisture pockets

Missing any of these steps in the first hour increases mold risk exponentially. Mold colonization begins within 24–48 hours in warm, moist environments—and San Marcos summers are humid.
