## What to Do in the First Hour After Water Damage

Your immediate actions directly influence restoration costs and insurance recovery. Here's the emergency protocol:

**Stop the water source** if it's safe. Turn off the main water valve if pipes burst. Cut power to affected areas if water is near electrical outlets or appliances. Do not re-enter flooded rooms if electrical hazards exist.

**Call a restoration company immediately.** Tamarack Restoration responds within 60 minutes in Encinitas. Early arrival means faster water extraction, reduced drying time, and lower likelihood of mold colonization.

**Document everything with photos and video.** Capture standing water, damaged personal property, and affected areas before cleanup begins. This documentation supports insurance claims.

**Move salvageable items to dry areas.** Furniture, electronics, and documents can sometimes be saved if moved quickly. Restoration teams can advise what's salvageable on arrival.

**Do not attempt DIY extraction.** Wet drywall, insulation, and subflooring hide moisture pockets that lead to mold. Professional equipment (dehumidifiers, air movers, moisture meters) is essential.

**Contact your insurance company.** Report the loss and mention you've engaged a professional restoration team. Ask about your deductible and coverage limits.

## How Professional Water Damage Restoration Works

[Water Damage Restoration Encinitas](/service-area/encinitas/water-damage-restoration/) follows a standardized process developed by the IICRC (Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification):

**Assessment and water extraction** (hours 1–4): Technicians identify the water source, categorize the damage (clean water, gray water, or sewage), and deploy submersible pumps and wet vacuums. In a typical Encinitas home, extraction takes 2–8 hours depending on volume.

**Structural drying** (days 1–7): Industrial dehumidifiers and air movers are positioned to evaporate moisture from walls, flooring, and framing. Technicians monitor moisture levels with meters to confirm drying progress. Incomplete drying is the leading cause of mold growth and secondary damage.

**Mold prevention and treatment** (ongoing): If mold spores are detected, affected materials are treated with antimicrobial solutions. Heavily contaminated materials (drywall, carpet, insulation) may be removed and replaced.

**Restoration and reconstruction** (weeks 2–8): Damaged drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and trim are replaced. Painting, refinishing, and reinstallation restore your home to pre-loss condition.

**Insurance coordination**: Documentation, photos, and repair estimates are provided to your adjuster. Tamarack Restoration works with your insurer to streamline the claims process.
